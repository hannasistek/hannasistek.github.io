ConvoLab

This project develops a human–AI interaction tool that allows users to practice difficult political conversations with people they disagree with. The system uses two coordinated AI agents: one that role-plays an anticipated interlocutor, and another that functions as a conversation coach, providing feedback and gentle nudges based on established principles of constructive dialogue and partisan bridging.
